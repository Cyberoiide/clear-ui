Timer screen: three directions

Job of the screen: start or stop the timer on a project, and check where today and this week went. The dark button is the primary action in each direction. Sample data: a freelance designer, Wednesday, 14:10.

Timer bar, day timeline, week bars Recommended

One timer bar on top, today drawn as a timeline with its entries below, this week as sorted bars beside it.
