Time tracker main screen: three directions

Job of the screen: start a timer on a project, stop it, and check where the day and the week went. The dark button is the primary action in each direction. Each one is drawn at desktop width and as a phone.

Timer bar, then the day, week beside it Recommended

One bar at the top is the timer; today's entries fill the page under it and the week's hours per project sit in a column on the right.
